<objective>
Close the player-to-enemy half of the combat loop: an enemy that is shot flinches, and an
enemy that is killed dies with a multi-frame animation and leaves a corpse on the floor
that the player can no longer shoot, while a kill tally counts the dead out of the total.

Plan 05-01 built the enemy and the damage entry point; plan 05-02 gave the player a gun
that calls it. This plan fills the documented 05-01 gap — the ENEM-04 hit reaction, death
animation and corpse, and the ENEM-05 kill count — so that after it lands a real player can
shoot an enemy, see it stagger, see it die properly, and see the tally move.

Purpose: an enemy that vanishes on death sells nothing. The multi-frame death and the
staggering pain reaction are what make combat read as combat, and the kill count is the
progression signal Phase 6's HUD and victory screen consume.

Output: five new enemy animation frames, the pain and death branches of the locked state
machine, corpse semantics, the kill tally, and the proofs for all of it.

BOUNDARY: rendering the kill count on screen is HUD-01/HUD-02 and belongs to Phase 6. This
plan produces the numbers and leaves them on Game.
</objective>
