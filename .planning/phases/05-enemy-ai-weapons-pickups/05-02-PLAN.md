<objective>
Give the player a working gun: a single-shot pistol and a spread-firing shotgun that deal
hitscan damage to the nearest alive enemy along the aim line, are stopped by walls, consume
ammo, refuse to fire when empty, and are presented with a bobbing viewmodel, a recoil kick
and a muzzle flash.

This expands outward from the 05-01 tracer along the opposite direction of the combat loop:
05-01 proved enemy-to-player, this plan proves player-to-enemy. After it lands, a real
player can shoot, hit, miss behind cover, run out of ammo and switch weapons.

Purpose: hitscan firing is the second half of the core value ("shoot weapons, manage
health/ammo"). The load-bearing correctness detail is the wall-stop — the research calls
"shooting through walls" out explicitly — so it gets a paired falsifiable proof.

Output: js/weapons.js, the fire and weapon-select intents, the shotgun/muzzle-flash
sprites, an ordered overlay seam on the raycaster, and tools/verify-weapons.cjs.

BOUNDARY: this plan does NOT implement the pain reaction, the death animation, the corpse
or the kill count (plan 05-03 owns ENEM-04/ENEM-05), and it does NOT render the HUD,
crosshair or ammo readout (Phase 6). It calls Enemies.hurt and stops there.
</objective>
